### Production Notes for Virality

**1. The Hook (0-3s):**
- **Why it works:** It immediately triggers fear and curiosity. The "Dot Com" comparison is universally understood.
- **Visual Tip:** Use high-contrast colors (Red for danger, Green for money) in the first 3 seconds.

**2. Pacing & Audio:**
- **Voiceover:** Speak 15% faster than your normal speaking voice. Use a "whisper-to-shout" dynamic—start calm, get louder at the "house of cards" part.
- **Music:** Use a "Phonk" or "Synthwave" beat that has a heavy drop right when you say "The bubble pops."

**3. The Retention Hack (The Loop):**
- **Trick:** At the very end (00:55), don't say "Thanks for watching." Instead, say: *"The worst part? The biggest bubble isn't even AI... it's [Blank]."*
- **Why:** This forces them to re-watch or check the comments to find the answer, boosting your engagement metrics.

**4. Captions:**
- **Style:** Use "Karaoke style" captions (word-by-word highlighting) in a bold font (like Montserrat or Impact). Color keywords like "BILLIONS" and "CRASH" in bright yellow or red.

**5. Hashtags:**
